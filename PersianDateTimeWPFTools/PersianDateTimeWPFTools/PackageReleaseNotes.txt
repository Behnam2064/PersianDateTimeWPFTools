### Bug Fixes

Fixed duplicate date change events when changing selected date/time values in:

* `PersianCalendarWithClock`
* `PersianDatePicker`
* `PersianDateTimePicker`

Also improved synchronization between the public properties and the internal calendar/clock controls.


This release introduces a fully standardized and reliable wheel interaction model for the Clock control:

- **Ctrl** now adjusts the hour
- **Shift** adjusts the minute
- **Ctrl + Shift** adjusts the second
- Removed **Alt** modifier usage due to WPF AccessKey/MenuMode conflicts
- Improved consistency of wheel behavior when interacting with the Canvas surface
- Internal refactoring for cleaner logic and easier future extension

These changes provide a more predictable and professional user experience while maintaining backward compatibility with existing applications.
